This web app is an AI-enabled, climate-smart service that closes the gap between pest forecasts and farm-level action. Integrating ILCYM phenology models with a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) interactive platform on Shiny, it translates complex climate data into localized IPM advice while keeping experts in the loop. To ensure inclusion, delivery is co-designed with women-led enterprises, cooperatives, and community intermediaries, solving gender-specific barriers to agency and finance. The platform links recommendations directly to verified local agro-dealers for purchasing biopesticides and traps. Built as a self-sustaining public-private architecture, it embeds translation capacity into national extension networks and private business models, driving behavioral change, avoiding crop losses, and establishing a scalable, self-funding risk management service.
